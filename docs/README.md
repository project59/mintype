# Mintype documentation

Mintype is a free note-taking app for documents and whiteboards. It runs in your browser, works offline, and encrypts your notes on your device before they are saved or synced.

These pages explain how to use Mintype and, in detail, how it protects your notes. The privacy pages are written for readers who want to check the claims for themselves, so they link to the source code wherever they can.

## Using Mintype

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): set up a password, save your recovery key, and learn how workspaces and pages fit together.
- [Documents and whiteboards](documents-and-whiteboards.md): the editor, its blocks, and the whiteboard canvas.
- [Sync and devices](sync-and-devices.md): Google Drive sync, adding a second device, and working offline.
- [Sharing with QuickShare](sharing.md): send someone a read-only, encrypted link to a page.
- [Import, export and backups](import-export.md): bring notes in from Notion or Markdown, and keep your own backups.

## Privacy and security

- [How Mintype protects your notes](security-overview.md): a plain-language summary. Start here.
- [Encryption in detail](encryption.md): the keys, algorithms, and what happens when you unlock, change your password, or recover your account.
- [What is stored where](data-storage.md): everything kept on your device, in Google Drive, and on Mintype's servers, and what each can read.
- [Limitations](limitations.md): what Mintype does not protect against.
- [Check it yourself](verify.md): how to confirm these claims with your browser's developer tools.
